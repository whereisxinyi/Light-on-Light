# Light-on-Light

> Translating fleeting thoughts into visual gifts.

## Concept

**Light on Light** is a web project that transforms fragments of everyday language into visual images.

It captures fleeting thoughts — a sudden sentence, an unfinished feeling, or a small question — and translates them into visual memories.

Although these moments are brief, they often carry personal meaning. Instead of letting them disappear in the flow of information, this project turns them into something visible and worth keeping.

---

## Background

We have many tools to record thoughts, but most simply store words.  
As image-making becomes more immediate and accessible, I began to explore:

**Can images become a way to respond to our thoughts?**

Light on Light explores how a simple piece of text can become a visual gift — a small artwork created for ourselves.

---

## Research Question

**How can we transform fleeting thoughts into meaningful memories?**

## Design Response

**By translating words into visual gifts.**

A thought becomes a sentence.  
A sentence becomes an image.  
An image becomes a memory.
